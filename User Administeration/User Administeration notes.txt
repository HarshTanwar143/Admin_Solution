**Scenario:**

* 👨‍💼 **Manager:** Gives **Admin Ravi** the **`security_admin`** role.
* 👨‍💻 **Ravi (Admin):** Normally works as an admin.
* 🔒 When Ravi needs to change security settings, he **elevates** to `security_admin`.
* ✅ After finishing, the elevated access is turned off.

**In short:** Permission is given once by a higher admin, and the user **activates (elevates)** it only when needed.
